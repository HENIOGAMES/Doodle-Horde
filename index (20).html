Doodle Horde

Someone's been doodling all over town, and the doodles have come to life. They're shambling through the streets toward you. Hold them off as long as you can.

  • Left thumb-stick: push up/down to walk, left/right to turn. Drag anywhere else to look around.
  • Hold FIRE to shoot; you aim at the middle of the screen. DODGE dashes, ↻ reloads.
  • Tap a gun along the top to switch. Tap the map for the town map.
  • Near a car? Tap Drive. The stick is gas, brake and steering.
  • Headshots do double damage. Watch the map: they come from every side.
  • ↑ ↓ walk, ← → turn (or WASD)
  • Move the mouse to look around; you always aim at the middle of the screen. Click to shoot (hold for the Rapidfire). Watch the map: they come from every direction.
  • 1–9 and 0 (or the scroll wheel) pick from 10 guns · M opens the town map · R reloads · Shift dodges · P pauses
  • Headshots do double damage. Every gun is yours from the start.
  • Walk up to a car and press E to drive it. Run them over!
